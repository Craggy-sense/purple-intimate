🔄 Official Policy

24-Hour Return & Exchange Policy

At Purple Intimate Lingerie, client hygiene and product purity are our strictest commitments. Because intimate lingerie, shapewear, and panties are personal hygienic items, we enforce the following exchange policy:

⏱️ 24-Hour Reporting Window:
Any size exchange or return request must be initiated within 24 hours of receiving your parcel.
  • All items must be completely unworn, unwashed, and in their original packaging.
  • All factory tags and plastic protective hygiene gusset liners must remain intact.
  • Items with perfume scents, makeup stains, or detached tags cannot be exchanged.
  • For health and hygiene safety, intimate adult toys and adhesive cups are strictly final sale.

How to Request an Exchange:
Send a photo of your receipt and the unworn item to our official WhatsApp line at 0794 672 167 within 24 hours of delivery. Our Kilimani store team will coordinate an immediate swap or invite you to visit Shop MF18 at The Circle Mall for an in-person fitting.
